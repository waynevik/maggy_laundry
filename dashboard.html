About Our Laundry

About the Business

Our laundry business provides professional clothing and household textile cleaning services. We are committed to providing reliable, quality and careful laundry services to every customer.

Our Services

  • Gown Cleaning
  • Household Textile Cleaning
  • Dry Cleaning
  • Pressing / Ironing Only
  • Wash / Cleaning Only
  • Wash + Iron

Customer Care

Every customer should be treated with respect, patience and professionalism.

  • Welcome customers politely.
  • Listen carefully to their needs.
  • Explain prices clearly.
  • Record customer information correctly.
  • Handle clothes carefully.
  • Confirm the customer's order before accepting it.
  • Confirm payment and any outstanding balance.
  • Keep customer information confidential.
  • Inform customers when their clothes are ready.
  • Resolve complaints politely and professionally.

Staff Responsibilities

  • Register every customer order.
  • Record the clothes received.
  • Check the customer's payment.
  • Update the laundry status.
  • Ensure the correct clothes are returned to the correct customer.
